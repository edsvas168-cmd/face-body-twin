    SCIENCE WITHOUT OVERPROMISES

    Evidence guides the story. Not an exact prediction.

    High sodium intake is relevant to fluid balance. Some studies link high-glycemic dietary patterns with acne in some people. Neither relationship gives us an accurate formula for how your specific face or body will look after one meal.

    Our calculator uses source-based nutritional data. Our displayed visual intensity follows clearly labeled product-design rules and is not an average measured human response.
