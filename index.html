A FEW IMPORTANT ANSWERS

Curious? Good.

Will the image show exactly what will happen to me?

No. This is a possible illustrative scenario, not a personalized medical prediction. Individual responses vary, and some suggested effects might not occur.

Do I have to upload my own photo?

No. You can use a prepared woman or man model. The current prototype also lets you preview a personal photo locally in your browser.

Is the face or body result already AI-generated?

Not yet. This prototype uses local demo graphics and a subtle display treatment. We are testing dedicated YouCam image-processing integration separately.

Where can I read the supporting information?

Research and health organizations are linked on this page and in the Studio results. Evidence about dietary patterns should not be interpreted as a guarantee about a single meal.
