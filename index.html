FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few things worth knowing.

Does a visualization predict exactly what will happen to me?

No. The result is an illustrative scenario based on general evidence and predefined demo behavior. Individual effects differ, and the depicted change may not happen.

Do I need to upload a photo?

No. You can choose a ready-made female or male demonstration model. The current Studio also supports a local photo preview.

Is this already a live YouCam AI transformation?

Not yet. The current Studio is a prototype with illustrative avatar visuals and unchanged local photo previews. The YouCam integration is a separate development phase.

How long might food-related puffiness last?

Mild puffiness may resolve within hours for some people, but fluid retention can last longer. The cause and personal factors matter; the app cannot predict your timing.
